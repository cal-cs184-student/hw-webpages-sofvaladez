Overview

This assignment builds a cloth simulator where a piece of cloth is modeled as a grid of point masses connected by springs. We simulate how the cloth moves under gravity, handle collisions with objects like spheres and planes, prevent the cloth from clipping through itself, and write shaders to make it look realistic.

By lowering the ks, the cloth behaves like loose, strechy fabric. It will fall very dramatically and will hang low, taking a while to settle if moved. By increasing the ks, the cloth will become very stiff. It will not change every much when moving.
If the density is very low, the cloth will be very light and will not be heavily impacted by gravity. It will fall very slowly and will have a flat resting space. With high density, the cloth will be very heavy and will give a similar visual effect to lowering ds, looking very 'saggy.'
Lowering damping makes the cloth almost frictionless, it will fall very fast and oscillate for a long time. It will look very chaotic. Increasing the damping will make the cloth move very slowly and smoothly. There will be minimal oscillation and will settle to its resting state easily and quickly.
